# Changelog

Versions follow [Semantic Versioning](https://semver.org). Each release is a `vX.Y.Z` tag on `main`; the web
deploys from `main`, and the Android and iOS store builds are made from the tag.

## 1.0.0 (unreleased)

The first public release: web (PWA), Android (Google Play) and iOS (App Store).

### Times
- Sehri, iftar and the five prayers for all 64 districts of Bangladesh and cities worldwide, calibrated to the
  Islamic Foundation Bangladesh timetable, with the calculation method and source shown.
- The 30-day Ramadan timetable with the three ashras, printable.
- Next-prayer countdown on Today; 12- or 24-hour clock with Bengali day periods.

### Worship
- Daily amal planner, prayer and Quran tracking, khatam progress and a Ramadan journal.
- Quran reader with Arabic, Bengali and English, and recitation audio.
- Sourced duas, a tasbih counter and a zakat calculator.

### Reminders
- Sehri, iftar and prayer reminders by web push and email, and as a calendar feed (ICS, Google Calendar).

### Everyone
- Works fully as a guest, with data kept on the device; an optional account keeps it across devices.
- Bengali first, with English; light and dark; works offline after the first visit.
- Privacy page, "Download my data" and "Delete account" in Settings.
- Free, with no ads. Donations are taken on the website only; the store apps show none.
